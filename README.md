# Wonek Video Studio — user documentation

Creators and small teams organizing several channels or turning product context into exportable video assets.

## At a glance

| Capability | What the product documents | Boundary to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Channel workspace | Organize media, scripts, thumbnails and publishing context per channel. | Organizing a channel does not guarantee reach or engagement. |
| Creative tools | Prepare hooks, briefs, captions, subtitles, voiceovers and thumbnail concepts. | Generated scripts and captions need editorial review, especially product claims and proper names. |
| AI processing | Supported local-model workflows and optional external provider integrations. | Local and external workflows have different setup, hardware, network and billing requirements. |

## Scope and provenance

Published by the product developer as first-party user documentation. It covers public behavior, examples and limitations; it does not contain the private application source, credentials or internal operational configuration. It is not an independent review, security audit or comparative benchmark.

Examples use synthetic or permission-cleared content. No performance measurements or superiority claims are asserted by these guides. Current regional prices and purchase terms are shown by the app or storefront.
